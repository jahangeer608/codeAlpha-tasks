02 Positions

We are looking for a motivated Software Developer to design, build and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code — working closely with cross‑functional teams to turn business requirements into robust software, across the full development lifecycle from planning to deployment.

01Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, test, and deploy scalable web and/or mobile applications.
  • Write clean, maintainable, well‑documented code following best practices.
  • Collaborate with product managers, designers, and QA to define and ship features.
  • Debug, troubleshoot, and resolve software defects and performance issues.
  • Participate in code reviews to maintain quality and share knowledge with the team.
  • Integrate third‑party APIs and services per project specifications.
  • Maintain and improve existing applications through updates and refactoring.
  • Contribute to technical documentation, including architecture diagrams.

02Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
  • 1–3 years of hands‑on experience in software development.
  • Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and OOP.
  • Experience with version control systems, particularly Git.
  • Internship or academic project experience is a plus.

03Required Skills

  • Proficiency in a modern language — JavaScript, Python, Java, or C#.
  • Experience with front‑end frameworks: React, Angular, or Vue.js.
  • Familiarity with back‑end frameworks: Node.js, Django, or .NET.
  • Working knowledge of relational and/or NoSQL databases.
  • Understanding of RESTful API design and integration.
  • Basic cloud platform knowledge (AWS, Azure, or GCP) is an advantage.
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies.

We are seeking a proactive HR Executive to manage day‑to‑day human resource operations — recruitment, employee relations, and HR administration — supporting the full employee lifecycle and fostering a positive workplace culture.

01Key Responsibilities

  • Manage end‑to‑end recruitment: postings, screening, interviews, onboarding.
  • Maintain employee records and HR databases per company policy.
  • Coordinate onboarding and orientation programs for new hires.
  • Assist in developing and implementing HR policies and procedures.
  • Handle employee queries on leave, benefits, payroll, and workplace policy.
  • Support performance management, including appraisals and feedback cycles.
  • Organize training and development sessions to build employee skills.
  • Ensure compliance with labor laws and company regulations.

02Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in HR Management, Business Administration, or related field.
  • 1–2 years of experience in an HR generalist or recruitment role.
  • Knowledge of local labor laws and HR best practices.
  • Professional HR certification (SHRM, CIPD) is a plus, not mandatory.

03Required Skills

  • Proficiency in MS Office and HRIS/HRMS software.
  • Strong knowledge of recruitment platforms and applicant tracking systems.
  • Understanding of compensation, benefits, and payroll processes.
  • Familiarity with performance management and engagement tools.
  • Good report‑writing and documentation skills.
